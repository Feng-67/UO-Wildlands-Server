## Custom Pets

### Named Pets

- **Named Pets** are unique creatures that do not ordinarily spawn in the world - they are accessed via Encounters - wave based spawns found in specific locations around the world with **100% chance** of the creature spawning once the Encounter is complete.
- **Named Pets** There are currently fifteen Named Pets to collect.
- **Named Pets** generally require **108 Taming skill** minimum

### Legendary Pets

- **Legendary Pets** have a random chance to spawn from specific regular tamable pets (**All 10% except Phoenix - 15% chance**) and are enhanced versions with variations such as lower starting slots, increased Hit Points, or changes to abilities.
- **Legendary Pets** are the best version of their pet type in terms of potential power when fully trained, and include creatures such as the Firesteed, Nightmare and many more. There are currently eight Legendary Pets to collect.
- All **Legendary Pets** require **120 Taming skill** minimum

## Mercenaries

Hired Mercenaries can be found at Jhelom Fighting Pits as hired NPCs.

- Costs 5000 GP per real day and is paid in advance - for example drop 35k GP on them for a week
- When it's time to pay their wage, they will tell you. If you don't pay, after a short time they disappear
- Mercenaries can be bonded with a Pet Bonding Potion found on the Ultima Store
- If a hireling dies in battle while not bonded, they are gone and you need to hire another one
- If a hireling is bonded and dies in battle, they can be resurrected with Bandages or Gift of Life and retain their gear
- If a hirelings pay in advance expires and they are not paid, they will disappear even if bonded
- Paladin, Fighter, (Ranger) Archer available - two of each Felucca and Trammel
- Animal Lore has been adjusted to accept using the skill on Mercenaries
- Control slots 4 to reflect increased strength but not as powerful as a fully trained pet
- Strength increased to 500-600
- Hit Points increased to 650 can survive tough fights but health must still be managed
- Dexterity increased to 200 fast enough to keep up while a player is mounted
- Adjusted so only have six skills which are better matched to class and all set to 120
- Increased all damage to 18-28
- All Damage Resistances set to 70
- Mercenaries with Healing have 100 Bandages, and the archer has 500 arrows

## Wave Spawns, Trials & Encounters

- **Wave Spawns** have been implemented at various Felucca and other locations
- **Automated Tiers:** Clearing specific waves triggers sequential difficulty increases within a set timeframe - some Wave Spawns are harder than others
- **Peerless Trials:** Successful completion of specific location wave sequences spawns a Peerless Boss directly - no peerless keys required. Eight Peerless Trials exist - one for each boss
- **Named Pet Encounters:** Specific location wave sequences serve as a spawn point for custom Named Pets - scaled according to pet quality. Named Pets are unique to Encounters - they do not spawn naturally in the wild. Fifteen Encounters currently exist in special world locations in Felucca, Ilshenar, Ter-Mur, Tokuno, and Eodon - ready to be discovered

## Corpse Retrieval Options

Options to retrieve all items on a player's corpse include the Undertaker's Staff that is purchasable on the Ultima Store and using the Corpse Retrieval Stone.

- Both options **DESTROYS ALL GOLD ON THE CORPSE** in the process
- The **Undertaker's Staff** costs 1000 Sov's (1 mil gold) and has 100 charges, costing the equivalent of **10K per retrieval**. The staff is a blessed item a character can carry and retrieves items from a corpse anywhere on the same map - for example anywhere in Felucca if standing in Felucca
- The **Corpse Retrieval Stone** costs **15K gold per retrieval**. When a player dies, they are prompted to use the Corpse Retrieval Stone and if so are teleported to it's location in New Haven. If the prompt is closed, a player can reopen it with the in-game command `[rezstone`. Resurrection is initiated by moving near the stone, and double clicking it opens the item retrieval option. It retrieves items from a corpse anywhere in the game - all maps.

## Animal Form

The restrictions on Ninjitsu Animal Form have been removed.

You can now remain in form while performing the following:

- **Full Spellcasting:** Cast any spell (Magery, Necro, Chivalry, etc.) without breaking form
- **Combat Masteries:** Use all Skill Masteries and Bardic Spellsongs
- **Special Moves:** Use all Weapon Abilities and Special Moves
- **Skill Usage:** Use restricted skills like Stealing or Meditation while transformed

**Note:** You still cannot "stack" transformations (e.g., you cannot cast Vampiric Embrace or Wraith Form while already in Animal Form)

## Townhouses

Added Town Houses, enabling the conversion of any static house in the world into a purchasable home.

- The house sign tooltip displays the status. Double click the sign to open the townhouse gump to purchase
- Items within the townhouse are tied to the map files so they can technically be deleted by admin but return on server restart. The area available should include one square on both bottom sides to allow for vendors or item placement.
- Updated the gump to allow ranged access to functions, and policy of a set selling property price of 250k and 1500 item limit for every Townhouse no matter the size, in line with normal housing.
- Players can secure items and containers; however, this must be done through the sign gump and commands as well as the advanced interior decorator does not function.
- *Every account can purchase up to 3 Townhouses. One account can own up to 3 regular houses, plus 3 town houses per character* Townhouses do not decay.
- They can be transferred for free to other players, sold for any price or abandoned. If an owner (character) is deleted or a player (account) leaves the game, items remain in position until released manually by GM. Secure containers display their current settings and can't be opened by other players.
- Town houses are not like houses or inns for logging out safely, but log out logic has been adjusted globally so logging out anywhere instantly removes the character from the game
- Owners can set access levels so Co-owners can add friends and change security, and friends can't change security
- Townhouses are private by default so other players do not have access to the area. This can be toggled to public, and owners have the option of setting friends, co-owners, as well as banning players (for example - the townhouse is set to private but you add a friend to allow them access, or set it to private and ban a player from the area)
- Added the ability to use almost all deed placable items such as a forge or loom, including Davies Locker, Steward and Mannequin
