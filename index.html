✨ What Makes UO Wildlands Unique?

  • 🛡️ PvE-Centric – PvP is disabled outside of guild wars and dueling arenas
  • 🌍 Increased World Spawns – Higher mob density, more high-end creatures, and rebalanced dungeons
  • 🐾 Custom Pets – Collect 16+ Named Pets and 8 Legendary Pets, each with unique abilities
  • 🏙️ Townhouses – Convert any static building into your own home
  • ⚔️ Mercenaries – Hire and bond NPC fighters to adventure alongside you
  • 🌊 Wave Spawns & Encounters – Dynamic battles with guaranteed rewards
  • 📦 Virtual Storage – Organize resources without cluttering your house
  • 🎨 Transmogrification – Change your gear's look without losing stats
  • 💀 Corpse Retrieval – Recover your items from anywhere in the world
  • 🔓 Free Skills – 19 skills that don't count toward your 720 cap
  • 🏆 30+ Champion Spawns – Easily accessible with 1-hour cooldowns
  • 🌍 Global Quality of Life – Recall almost anywhere, instant logout, and more

📖 Complete Feature List

Everything custom about UO Wildlands — systems, world changes, PvP/PvE settings, skill changes, and quality-of-life updates.

PvP & Player Stealing Disabled

PvP, snooping, and stealing from other players is disabled. PvP only in Guild Wars and Dueling Arenas. VvV and Factions disabled.

Seasonal Content

Khaldun, Treasures of Tokuno, Treasures of Doom, Kotl Dungeon, Krampus, Rising Tide, and Fellowship — all permanently enabled. Plunder Beacon rewards go straight to your backpack.

Veteran Rewards

Disabled and moved to the Ultima Store for Sov's. Starting stat cap raised from 225 to 230. All account age restrictions removed.

Custom Pets

15 Named Pets (108 Taming min) obtained through Encounters, and 8 Legendary Pets (120 Taming min) with a 10–15% spawn chance from specific tamables.

Public Moongates

Dungeon Moongate for one-way access to nearly all dungeons. New moongates in Nujelm, Serpent's Hold, and atop New Haven Bank.

Global Banking

Account-wide bank gold and Sovereigns. Cleaner banker gump, upgraded [wallet display, and the Platinum Rule (1 billion gold = 1 platinum in trade).

World Changes

Lockpicking chests, vendor tiles in Haven, decorated cities, farmable cotton, dungeon shortcuts, solo Exodus ritual, and dozens of small adjustments across both facets.

Mercenaries

Hire Paladin, Fighter, or Archer NPCs at Jhelom Fighting Pits. 5000 GP/day, bondable, resurrectable, and geared for high-end content.

Felucca Spawns

Rebalanced dungeon density across Felucca and Ilshenar. Final rooms stocked with Balrons, Ancient Wyrms, and other high-end mobs. Low-tier spawns swapped for high-tier counterparts.

Wave Spawns, Trials & Encounters

Automated tiered waves, eight Peerless Trials with no key requirement, and 15 Named Pet Encounters spread across Felucca, Ilshenar, Ter-Mur, Tokuno, and Eodon.

Technical & Global Changes

Boss HP normalized (Melisande to 50k), Medusa and Stygian Dragon drop peerless ingredients, improved loot profiles, and rare drops from Treasures Of tables.

Exploration-Based Testing

Feature locations are not documented on purpose. Scout Felucca and beyond to discover wave triggers, pet habitats, and hidden encounters.

Enhanced Training Dummies

Dummies train to 90, Advanced Dummies to 120 for Swords/Mace/Fencing. Archery Buttes to 90. Pickpocket Dips to 120 in Stealing.

Player Global Chat & Commands

Global chat plus a full suite of QoL commands — [mystats, [wallet, [myhouses, [rezstone, [bondinfo, and more.

Corpse Retrieval Options

Undertaker's Staff (same-map retrieval) and the New Haven Corpse Retrieval Stone (all-map retrieval). Both destroy all gold on the corpse.

Free Skills

19 skills don't count toward the 720 cap — Alchemy, Arms Lore, Begging, Camping, Cartography, Cooking, Detect Hidden, Fishing, Forensics, Herding, Hiding, Inscribe, Item ID, Lockpicking, Lumberjacking, Mining, Remove Trap, Taste ID, Tracking.

Animal Form

Ninjitsu Animal Form restrictions removed — full spellcasting, Combat Masteries, Spellsongs, Special Moves, and Stealing all work in form. No stacking transformations.

Townhouses

Convert any static house into a purchasable home. 250k gold, 1500 item limit, no decay. Up to 3 per account. Available across nine Trammel cities.

Champion Spawns

30+ spawns with tighter mob ranges, 1-hour cooldowns, recall restrictions removed, and instant teleport via the Champion Monolith. Includes Orcish Stronghold and Beholder.

Skill Changes

120/720 caps, 260 stat cap, +25 base HP. Faster self-bandage, no instrument durability drain on Spellsongs, 3-minute mastery switching. Reworked Magery/Mystic summons and commandable Mirror Images.

City Buffs

Permanent city stone buffs across all nine cities. Governors disabled, loyalty wait time reduced to 1 day.

General Updates

Instant logout anywhere, unique character names, 100% BOD banking, ground item decay in 1 hour, 1250 passive Felucca Luck, expanded pet lore, recall to boat-marked runes, and dozens more QoL changes.

Windows Setup

The game world is already built and ready to go. Simply point the server at your Ultima Online install and start.

  1. Download the server
    Click the green Code button on the GitHub repo, then choose Download ZIP. Extract the ZIP to a folder like your Desktop.
  2. Tell the server where your Ultima Online game is installed
    Open the Config folder → open DataPath.cfg with Notepad → change the path to match your UO install (e.g., C:\Ultima Online Classic) → save.
  3. Start the server
    Double-click ServUO.exe. If SmartScreen warns you, click More info → Run anyway. Wait for "Listening on 127.0.0.1:2593".
  4. Log in and play
    Open your UO client (ClassicUO, TazUO, Orion) → connect to 127.0.0.1 port 2593 → log in with Demon / admin1.

GM Commands

Once logged in as Demon, type [mycommands or [gm for a gump. Type [admin for standard ServUO server owner commands.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or newer
  • A valid Ultima Online classic client installed

Troubleshooting

If ServUO.exe fails to launch, copy the error text (Ctrl+C in console) and paste it into a chatbot like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for troubleshooting.
